How these numbers were computed

Every output traces to this record

Mass balance

Concentrations are drawn from lognormal distributions fitted to the occurrence database. Retention in the top 15 cm depends on soil class. 1,500 Monte Carlo draws per result.

Where the uncertainty comes from

Share of variance in the selected result. The sampling design in the project targets the largest term first.

Scope. This prototype shows the intended form of the comparative mass-input calculator: mass input and environmental fate, with uncertainty and a full computation record. It does not estimate exposure or health risk, and it does not state whether a field or product complies with any regulation. Values are illustrative placeholders standing in for the project database and models.
